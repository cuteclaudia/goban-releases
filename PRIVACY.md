# Goban (碁盤) privacy statement

*Last updated 29 October 2026. Goban is CuteClaudia's spreadsheet app, part of the Kōbō suite.*

**In short:** Goban has no account, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
reporting. Your files stay on your computer, or in storage you choose. CuteClaudia doesn't receive any
of your data.

## Your files

Goban opens and saves files where you choose. It does not upload, copy or send them anywhere.
Settings and recent-file lists are kept in Goban's settings folder on your computer.

## Updates

- **Copies from GitHub** (the installer or portable download): When it starts, Goban asks GitHub for the newest **published** release of `cuteclaudia/goban-releases`, and offers it to you. Nothing about you, your files or your computer is sent; GitHub sees an ordinary web request (your IP address), under GitHub's privacy statement.
- **Copies from the Microsoft Store**: the Store installs and updates Goban; Goban doesn't
  check GitHub at all. Microsoft's privacy statement covers the Store.

## Links

Links in Help and About (claudiagilbert.com, the releases page, feedback) open in your browser
**only when you click them**.

## Automation (optional)

Goban can be controlled by scripts and AI agents through a control channel and an MCP server.
They are **off** unless you start them yourself, listen only on your own computer, and need a
secret key that only you have.

## Children

Goban collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this statement are published here, with the date at the top. Questions or concerns:
[open an issue](https://github.com/cuteclaudia/goban-releases/issues/new) (please don't include
personal information or files), or contact CuteClaudia through claudiagilbert.com.
